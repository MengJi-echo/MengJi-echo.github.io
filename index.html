Biography

Meng Ji received his Ph.D. in 2020 from Nankai University (advisor: Professor Xueliang Li) and his M.S. in 2017 from Xiamen University (advisor: Professor Xian'an Jin). He is currently a Lecturer at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Tianjin Normal University. His research interests focus on graph theory, particularly graph connectivity and Ramsey theory. In 2022, he received the IMU Kovalevskaya Grant Award for travel support. He was awarded the China Scholarship Council (CSC) 2023 National High-level Research Scholar, Visiting Scholar, and Postdoctoral Program.
